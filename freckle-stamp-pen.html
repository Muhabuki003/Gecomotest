Questions, answered.

How long does the freckle look take?⌄
About a minute for a full scattered look across the nose and cheeks. Most of that is softening the edges with a fingertip.
Does it look natural or stamped?⌄
Five dots land offset from each other rather than in a line, and tapping the edges with a fingertip blurs the outline. Using more than one shade across the face reads most natural.
Is it waterproof?⌄
The formula is water-resistant, so it holds through a normal day out. It removes with cleanser or makeup remover.
Which shade should I choose?⌄
01 is the lightest honey tone and 04 is the deepest brown. If you are between two, the lighter one usually reads more natural on bare skin.
Will it work on any skin tone?⌄
Four shades cover light honey through deep brown. Mixing two shades across the face gives the most natural result on any tone.
How much product is in one pen?⌄
1.2 ml. A little pressure is all that is needed, so the pen lasts a long time.
Will it smudge under makeup?⌄
Apply the freckles onto a base that has settled, then set with a light powder if you wear powder. Pressing too hard is the usual cause of smudging.
How do I remove it?⌄
Cleanser or makeup remover. No scrubbing needed.
