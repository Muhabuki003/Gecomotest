Questions, answered.

How do the boots fit?⌄
They fit true to size with a roomy round toe, and are available in EU 35–41. If you plan to wear thick socks or you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up.
What are they made of?⌄
A soft fleece lining, a fluffy fold-over faux-fur cuff and a thick platform sole. The upper is a smooth, easy-wipe finish in Apricot or Khaki.
Are they water-resistant?⌄
They're built for cold, dry winter weather — the thick platform sole keeps you off wet ground. We don't recommend deep puddles or heavy rain.
How should I clean them?⌄
Wipe the upper with a soft, damp cloth and let them air dry away from direct heat. Do not machine wash or tumble dry.
Where do you ship, and how fast?⌄
We ship worldwide, free, with every order. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Once yours is on its way you can follow it on our Track Order page.
