Questions, answered.

How long do they take to put on?⌄
Matching the sizes is the longest part. Once you have matched them, pressing the set on takes a few minutes.
Do the flowers fall off?⌄
The 3D flowers are formed as part of the tip rather than glued on as a separate sticker, so there is no edge to catch and peel.
What adhesive do I need?⌄
Any press-on adhesive works — nail glue or adhesive tabs. Whatever you already use with press-ons is fine.
Do they come in different sizes?⌄
Yes. You get 24 tips across 12 sizes, so most nails will have a match.
Can I file or shorten them?⌄
Yes, file the free edge to shorten. Take it slowly around the 3D flowers.
How do I take them off?⌄
Soak in warm soapy water or use remover, then gently lift from the side. Stored carefully, the tips can be worn again.
Are they reusable?⌄
Yes — filed and stored with care, the set can be worn more than once.
