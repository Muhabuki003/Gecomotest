Questions, answered.

Is it like a brow pencil?⌄
No — a pencil draws on the skin between the hairs. This is a tinted gel on a spoolie: it coats the hairs themselves, so the brow looks fuller rather than drawn on.
Does it really hold all day?⌄
The formula is waterproof and sets the hairs in place. It holds through sweat and humidity and removes with warm water and cleanser or makeup remover.
Which shade should I choose?⌄
Black for true black to very dark brows; Brown for blonde through mid-brown. If you are between the two, Brown reads more natural on most people.
How much product is in a tube?⌄
4 g, which lasts months of daily use — you only need a light pass per brow.
Will it make my brows stiff?⌄
It sets the shape, so there is some hold. Wipe the spoolie on the tube rim first and work in one light pass to keep it soft.
Can I use it over brow pencil or powder?⌄
Yes. Comb the gel through last to set whatever you have already applied.
What is the Brow Playbook?⌄
Our free digital guide to finding your brow shape and matching your shade. It is included with every order and you can download it from the site.
How do I remove it?⌄
Warm water and cleanser, or any makeup remover. No scrubbing needed.
