Questions, answered.

How long do I leave it on?⌄
Around 10 to 20 minutes. If the layer tears when you peel it, give it a few more minutes next time — it had not set all the way through.
Does it actually peel off in one piece?⌄
When the layer has set properly, it lifts from the corner in one piece. Stretching it slowly from the corner works better than pulling straight up.
How do I stop the peel breaking up?⌄
Exfoliate first, let balm settle, and paint a slightly thicker layer past the lip line. A thin layer is the usual reason a peel tears.
Will it dry my lips out?⌄
Let a little balm settle into the lip before you paint, and finish with the glaze in the set.
Which shade should I choose?⌄
Purple Red is the coolest and deepest, Cherry Red is the classic red, Terracotta Orange is the warm one, and Coral Pink is the softest.
How long does the colour stay on?⌄
It stays through everyday wear, eating and drinking included. Very oily food will always soften a lip tint faster — that is true of the category, not just this one.
Can I wear it under the glaze?⌄
Yes — the glaze goes on over the tint. Wear the tint bare for a softer flush, or add the glaze for shine.
How do I remove it?⌄
With any oil-based cleanser or makeup remover. The tint comes off with the same thing you would use on a long-wear lipstick.
